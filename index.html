HAS

A private household assistant that lives on your phone.

HAS keeps the small things of a household in one place: your tasks and grocery lists, your appointments, the places you go. You can type or talk to it, ask what you have on a day, where you were on a Tuesday, or when you last bought milk, and it answers from your own records.

What it does

  • Tasks and grocery lists, with repeats, reminders and a history of what you bought.
  • A calendar that can show the calendars already on your phone, and your Google or Outlook calendar if you connect one.
  • A private location history, kept on the phone, so you can ask about a day and see your stops on a map.
  • A conversation with HAS about all of it. If you let it, HAS learns what matters to you over time, and you can erase that at any time.

Where your data lives

Your lists, calendar, places and conversations are stored on your phone. When you ask HAS something, your message and the details needed to answer it are sent to the HAS server and to the AI provider that writes the answer, and are not kept there. The details are in the privacy notice.
